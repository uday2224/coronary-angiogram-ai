# FDA Regulatory Notice & Clinical Disclaimer

## Investigational Use Only (IUO) Notice
> **CAUTION — Investigational Device. Limited by Federal (or United States) law to investigational use.**
> *(Conforming with 21 CFR 812.5 labeling requirements)*

---

### 1. Intended Purpose
The **Coronary Angiogram AI Workstation** is a scientific research prototype and clinical investigation tool designed to evaluate deep learning algorithms on standard coronary fluoroscopic angiograms.

This software application is intended strictly for:
1. Academic, scientific, and engineering evaluation of convolutional and segmentation neural network architectures.
2. Exploratory computer-assisted quantification of vessel boundaries, minimal lumen diameter (MLD), and atherosclerotic plaque morphology.
3. Interactive 3D visualization and education regarding coronary anatomy and flow dynamics.

---

### 2. Regulatory Boundaries & Limitations
- **Not an FDA Cleared Diagnostic Device**: This platform has **NOT** been evaluated, approved, or cleared by the United States Food and Drug Administration (FDA) under 510(k), De Novo, or Premarket Approval (PMA) pathways, nor has it received CE-mark certification as software as a medical device (SaMD).
- **Mandatory Clinical Confirmation**: Predictions, segmentations, probability scores, stenosis gradings, and Grad-CAM visualizations generated by this platform are research estimates derived from models trained on the ARCADE (Automatic Region and Lesion Assessment for Coronary Artery Disease) dataset. They must **never** be used as the sole basis for diagnosing coronary artery disease, planning percutaneous coronary intervention (PCI), or altering patient medical management without direct evaluation and confirmation by a board-certified interventional cardiologist.
- **2D-to-3D Scientific Limitations**: 3D virtual heart and artery interior models represent synthetic anatomical reference reconstructions based on standard human coronary geometry. They do not constitute patient-specific computed tomography angiography (CTA) segmentations, computational fluid dynamics (CFD), or invasive fractional flow reserve (FFR) measurements.
